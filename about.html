25 Years In Action

Founded in 1999, the Lahore Media Council has been the cornerstone of ethical journalism in the heart of Punjab. For over two decades, we have championed press freedom, nurtured investigative reporting, and provided a unified platform for media professionals across Lahore.

Our mission is to uphold the highest standards of truth, accuracy, and fairness. We believe that a well-informed public is the foundation of a strong democracy. Through training workshops, press briefings, and advocacy, we empower journalists to tell stories that matter.

From the bustling newsrooms of old Lahore to the digital frontiers of today, the Council remains a steadfast guardian of journalistic integrity and a voice for the voiceless.
